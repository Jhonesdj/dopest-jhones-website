About.

Dopest Jhones started spinning records at Lagos house parties before amapiano's log drums took over dance floors from Joburg to London. A decade and a few hundred sets later, the formula hasn't changed: horns up top, bass on the floor, and a set that's built live off the crowd, not off a laptop.

Every mix moves through afrobeats, amapiano, and the records that sit between them — always mixed for the room that's actually in front of the booth.
